# 🧘‍♀️ Stillpoint (Meditation) — Tempo Shift

> [!summary]+ 🧭 On this page (no links)
> TL;DR · Effect (two modes) · Limits & Interactions · Order of Operations · Examples · Acceptance checks · Lore · Changelog

> [!tldr] 💎 **TL;DR — What this gives you**
> **Use:** **1×**, regains after **two Long Rests**.  
> **Windows:**  
> • **Pre‑Initiative Shift** — after **initiative is rolled** but **before** the first turn begins, **move up to your speed** and **draw or stow one item**.  
> • **Between‑Turns Shift** — **between any two creatures’ turns**, **move up to your speed** **and** take **one Use an Object** action (levers, doors, buttons, syringes, etc.).  
> **It is not a reaction.** Movement is **normal movement** (not teleportation): **difficult terrain applies; you provoke Opportunity Attacks** as usual.  
> **No attacks or spells** as part of Stillpoint; this is a **tempo/positioning tool**.

---

## ⚙️ Effect — two precise windows
^effect

### 1) Pre‑Initiative Shift *(before the first turn)*
After initiative is rolled but **before** the first creature’s turn starts, you may **move up to your speed** and **draw or stow one item** as part of that movement.
- This is **neither an action nor a reaction**.
- You **can’t** attack, cast a spell, or take **Use an Object** here.
- You **can’t** use this mode if you are **surprised**.

### 2) Between‑Turns Shift *(mid‑round seam)*
Between any two creatures’ turns, you may **move up to your speed** and take **one Use an Object** action (e.g., pull a lever, press a switch, open/close, inject a dose).
- This special **Use an Object** is permitted **outside your turn** by this feature.
- It **does not** activate magic items unless that item’s rules explicitly use **Use an Object**.
- You **can’t** attack or cast a spell as part of this feature.

> [!tip] 🎯 Position, don’t pummel
> Stillpoint exists to **beat timing**—to **reach the panel**, **clear a doorway**, or **fix a sightline**—not to increase DPR.

---

## 📏 Limits & Interactions
^limits

- **Recharge:** once used, you regain Stillpoint after **two Long Rests**.
- **Not a reaction:** does **not** spend your reaction; does **not** qualify as a reaction for features that require one.
- **Movement is normal:** this is **not teleportation**. Apply **difficult terrain**, **speed 0**, **prone**, **grappled**, **restrained**, and other movement limits normally. If your **speed is 0**, you can’t move with Stillpoint (you may still take the **Use an Object** in the Between‑Turns mode).
- **Opportunity Attacks:** leaving a hostile creature’s reach **provokes** as normal. **Surprised** creatures can’t take reactions until the end of their first turn, so they can’t OA you during **Pre‑Initiative Shift**.
- **No Dash/Disengage/Attack/Cast:** Stillpoint never grants those actions. **Ongoing** speed modifiers (e.g., *longstrider*) **do apply** to the distance you can move.
- **Concentration:** unchanged by taking Stillpoint itself (concentration can still be broken by damage or other events as normal).
- **No chaining:** Don’t chain Stillpoint in the **same instant** with other out‑of‑turn movement features. You may benefit from **ongoing** movement buffs already in effect.
- **Instant long rest (Chaos Row 22):** Does **not** count toward Stillpoint’s “two Long Rests” recharge.

---

## 🧮 Order of Operations
^ops

- **Pre‑Initiative:** *Roll initiative → (optional) Stillpoint → first turn begins.*  
- **Between‑Turns:** *End of a creature’s turn → (optional) Stillpoint → next creature’s turn begins.*  
In both modes, resolve **movement** first, then any permitted **object interaction**. Apply OAs and movement costs normally.

## 🧪 Examples
^examples

> [!example]+ 🚪 **Door gamble (pre‑init)**  
> After initiative, you **sprint to wedge the blast door** before turrets come online. No attacks, no casting—just positioning and one draw/stow during the move.

> [!example]+ 🧷 **Circuit break (between turns)**  
> Between the guard’s turn and yours, you **step to the sigil plate** and **Use an Object** to pop the latch. You provoke OAs if you leave reach.

> [!example]+ 🧱 **Line‑of‑sight flip**  
> You slip to **hard cover** between turns so the round‑opening volley doesn’t have you in the open when it fires.

## ✅ Acceptance checks (adversarial quick‑tests)
^accept

- **Surprised at start:** You cannot use **Pre‑Initiative Shift** if you are **surprised**.  
- **Grappled:** With **speed 0**, you **can’t move** using Stillpoint; in **Between‑Turns**, you **may** still take **Use an Object**.  
- **OAs happen:** Moving out of reach **provokes OAs** (unless the creature can’t take reactions).  
- **No free attacks:** You cannot **attack or cast** during either mode.  
- **Magic items:** You can operate an item **only** if its text says it uses **Use an Object**; otherwise not.  
- **Recharge:** You use Stillpoint in Scene A; you must complete **two Long Rests** before you can use it again.
