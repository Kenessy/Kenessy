Act A · Scene 1

Null Meridian

You are dead. You know this because everything is wrong.

System OFF — No rules, no dice, no danger

Cold nothing gives way to stone under your hands.

A circular platform hangs in open dark, its edge traced by a dull red glow. Around you, a few blurred shapes shudder and drag themselves upright, bodies half-formed, as if something is still deciding what you're all supposed to be.

You have no heartbeat. No breath. No skin you can trust. But you have weight, and the floor is solid and cool beneath you.

◆ ◆ ◆

What do you do first?

Your outline flickers. For a heartbeat you see fingers — real, detailed, yours — and then they blur into shadow again, cycling through two or three versions of a hand you almost recognise. When you focus on what you want to be, the form leans that way, then collapses back to smoke.

You are a soul, not a body. Identity is still in flux. Whatever you were, it hasn't been decided yet.

The platform is rough stone or concrete — circular, maybe thirty feet across, no railings. Beyond the rim: nothing. A featureless dark that swallows distance. The thin red line at the edge doesn't behave like light or metal. It just is. If you stare long enough into the void, you almost see twin red lines in the distance, like rails. Then you blink, and they're gone.

There is structure out there. Something is counting. Something is turning. But it isn't reachable yet.

Your voice rings out — too clean, too clear, every syllable sharp in the emptiness. The words travel into the dark and come back as a soft, delayed echo from no direction you can point to. No answer. But after a few calls, the distant mechanical rhythm — that slow clack-clack-clack underneath everything — tightens, as if noticing.

Contact with the void is one-way. But when you speak to the other shapes on the platform, voices sound normal. Conversation between you is real.

Each step toward the rim feels like pushing through invisible resistance. The edge seems both near and far. You push through — step off — and there's a single lurch of falling into cold dark. Your stomach drops. Then your foot hits stone again, right where you started. From the outside, the others saw you blink from edge to centre. No fall. No gap.

You cannot leave. Space is looped around the platform. This place is a cage dressed as a waiting room.

◆ ◆ ◆

Something stirs beneath the blankness. Not a memory — memories are gone, rubbed off like text from a wet page. But there's a residue. An emotional shape left behind by whatever ended you.

At the end, you remember feeling mostly...

The anger flares like a pilot light — small, steady, refusing to go out. You don't remember who or what, but the shape of violence lingers. Something was taken. Something was unfinished. Your shadow-form tightens, edges sharpening for a heartbeat before blurring back.
A bone-deep weariness that has nothing to do with bones. You carried something too heavy for too long, and when you finally set it down, you set yourself down with it. Your shadow-form sags, loose and undefined, as if it's forgotten what tension feels like.
A quiet knowing. The end didn't surprise you. Maybe you even arranged it. Your shadow-form is still — unnervingly still — as if it accepted this shape long before arriving here. The absence of panic is its own kind of horror.
A stubborn warmth, absurd given the circumstances. You died mid-sentence, mid-reach, mid-promise. Your shadow-form keeps leaning forward, straining toward something that isn't on this platform. Not yet.
◆ ◆ ◆

Time passes. Or doesn't. You can't tell anymore.

The mechanical rhythm beneath the silence changes. Tighter. Closer. The red line at the platform's rim brightens — just slightly — and for the first time since waking, the darkness out there has direction.

Something is coming down the line.

And it's on schedule.
