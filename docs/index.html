ALERT Axes

Atmosphere

6 / 6 - Phenomenal

Lived-in stations, gunfire-lit frontlines, lighter-lit stealth routes, ghosts, surface exposure, and industrial decay make Metro feel dense, hostile, and inhabited. The only thing scarier than an abandoned station is one that is not abandoned.

Loop

5 / 6 - Excellent

Mode rotation keeps the authored route active: station, tunnel, surface, stealth, horror, D6, Tower.

Endurance

5 / 6 - Excellent

The run holds pace well; Library stress and first-time route-search friction are the main drains.

Runtime

6 / 6 - Phenomenal

Redux feels product-ready in this run; the local Tower/FOV capture issue remains disclosed.

Transparency

5 / 6 - Excellent

Most rules are readable, though mutants, Librarian spacing, and turret moments lower the ceiling.

Overlays

Agency + Cohesion

Agency

4 / 6 - Solid

Metro gives bounded tactical and route agency, but it remains an authored linear journey.

Cohesion

6 / 6 - Phenomenal

Writing, atmosphere, rules, levels, horror, and military endgame point in one direction.
