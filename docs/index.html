What it is

Players begin as dead souls in the Null Meridian, board the infernal Red Line, sign Lucifer's contract in the Sundown Saloon, and wake in a broken Earth trapped inside a paused double apocalypse.

Scene-first play

Shared visual scenes, sensory framing, and hard narrative choices land before mechanical density.

Infernal bureaucracy

Golden Tickets, inspectors, etiquette pamphlets, and contracts turn Hell into a transport system instead of generic fire-and-brimstone noise.

Second life with cost

Revival is rough, bodies are printed damaged, and the mission is not free heroism. It is a bargain.
