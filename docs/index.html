T2

Why the badge lands here

Metro 2033 Redux is a low-freedom, high-texture-density survival FPS. It wins through authored pressure, not systemic freedom. That makes 86 defensible and keeps 90+ out of reach.

Works because

The run proves a strong chain: Exhibition density, Bourbon survival guidance, Khan invisible rules, Frontline route agency, Library horror, D6 machinery, and Tower callback.

Held back by

Agency is bounded, scarcity is softened on replay-normal settings, and mutant, Librarian, turret, bug, and Tower caveats keep the verdict adversarial.

Know before buying

This is staged, linear, cinematic survival FPS. Buy for atmosphere, pressure, horror pacing, and authored setpieces. Do not buy expecting open RPG agency or sandbox systems.

Best fit

Atmosphere-first players who want a theatrical action-horror FPS with enough faction and survival texture to feel RPG-adjacent without becoming a sandbox.
