## Minimal review brief

We are reviewing **Horizon Zero Dawn Complete Edition** as a solo, story-first summer immersion game. The central question is whether its world mystery, Aloy's journey, machine-hunting combat, and visual exploration can carry the lighter roleplay, weaker human combat, and open-world checklist friction for a player looking for a Witcher / Mass Effect / Dragon Age-adjacent experience without Soulslike punishment or co-op dependency.

This is not being treated as a party RPG, romance RPG, branching narrative, survival game, Soulslike, or mandatory 100% grind. The first run exists to experience and review the game. The 79/79 route is a separate mastery decision after credits.

## Voice-mode capture contract

During voice play, Codex maintains the files rather than relying on conversational memory:

1. Every substantive spoken observation is appended verbatim to `raw-capture.md` before interpretation.
2. During an active long session, `live-state.md` and the current session entry receive durable checkpoints after meaningful beats and at least every few minutes of incoming commentary.
3. Raw wording is never rewritten or deleted. Corrections are appended.
4. Structured claims go to `session-log.md`; hypotheses and counterevidence go to `review-board.md`.
5. Publishable prose is derived later in `processed-playthrough.md` and must link back to raw/session evidence.
6. Unknowns stay marked `?`; Codex does not fill gaps from memory or guides.
7. At session close, the working folder is committed and synced. Long sessions may receive additional remote checkpoint commits.

Voice commentary can be completely natural. Useful signals include what just happened, what felt good or annoying, a choice made, a machine or quest that stood out, a difficulty spike, a technical issue, a screenshot moment, or simply whether the desire to continue rose or fell.

Current interaction mode is **silent dictation capture**. Codex does not verbally acknowledge routine observations. It responds only to an explicit question, an urgent technical risk, or a genuinely time-sensitive spoiler-free decision; all other substantive commentary is still written durably.
