# INV-SNAP-008 — Krauta / Mission Log / Relay Repair / Androbot Reboot

Source session: S01 camp investigation and repair loop  
Spoiler level: spoiler-marked  
Raw source: `raw-capture.md`, Raw Intake 010

## Observed state

- The unresponsive doctor is clarified as Dr. Krauta.
- Yasna uses / takes Krauta's cable or radio connection to restore communication.
- Base/Novik says they had heard Yasna for roughly two hours, but Yasna could not hear them until now.
- Base/Novik asks Yasna to examine Krauta.
- Krauta is unresponsive: sitting, swaying, mumbling, and not meaningfully interactive.
- Current goals become clearer: find remaining people, recover a mission log, determine lander coordinates, and investigate where the lander should land.
- Yasna finds the mission log in the tent room next to the doctor.
- Mission log indicates Dr. Gorsky moved away from the research sector.
- Mission log contains BA-23-16 / landing-coordinate-like data, a possible landing place, camp marker, and a `structures?` note.
- Mission log sample analysis includes nickel, iron, manganese, beryllium, and titanium, and rejects or doubts a simple giant nickel-iron meteor explanation because the shape of structures contradicts it.
- Yasna is directed to take or use a detector / X-ray-like device from a large equipment chest.
- Novik/Base tries to restart the android remotely but fails.
- Yasna notices the relay antenna is fried and finds it strange because an ordinary sandstorm should not cause that damage.
- Yasna searches boxes for a replacement relay and successfully reboots the android.
- The android can now watch over Dr. Krauta.

## Unknowns

- What damaged the relay antenna if an ordinary sandstorm could not?
- What are the marked structures?
- Why do material samples and structural shapes contradict a meteor-impact hypothesis?
- Where did Dr. Gorsky go, and why did he leave the research sector?
- Is BA-23-16 a coordinate, route marker, sector, landing site, or object designation?
- Does the detector reveal hidden structures, radiation, material composition, or biological/non-biological traces?
- Is the android simply damaged, or affected by the same phenomenon as Krauta?

## Working hypothesis

The camp loop converts passive mystery into active investigation. The key pattern is now: regain communication -> parse mission log -> update objectives -> diagnose equipment failure -> scavenge parts -> restore machine function. The fried relay is a clean anomaly because the game explicitly frames normal sandstorm damage as insufficient, forcing a stronger unknown cause.

## AI training value

Useful for:

- causal repair-chain modeling;
- mission-objective extraction from fragmented notes;
- material-analysis contradiction tracking;
- distinguishing ordinary environmental damage from anomalous damage;
- human-machine state comparison: Krauta stupor vs android failure;
- tool/task planning: identify fault, locate replacement, apply repair;
- world-state update after communication restoration.

This is not a dialogue-choice decision, but it is a strong agency/action sequence.
