## Observed state

- Flashback takes place on a ship, presumably before the current desert sequence.
- The crew says the Invincible is coming to Regis III.

- It has destructive capability strong enough to melt mountains with lasers.

- The crew goes down to the planet before Invincible arrives.

## Working hypothesis

The flashback creates the first strong scientific contradiction: the mission treats Regis III as biologically empty, but the story positions Yasna's medical/biological expertise as important. The likely thematic pressure is not simply “is there life?” but “what counts as life, danger, or intelligence?”
