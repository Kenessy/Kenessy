## Scene B2 — Chapel of the Second Dawn — Sanctuary & Yard

> [!summary] **SCENE B2 — Chapel of the Second Dawn — Sanctuary & Yard**  
> TYPE: Explore, Puzzle, Talk  
> LENGTH: Medium  
> DANGER: Risky, Homebase  
> PURPOSE: Intro, Setup  
> TEACHING: Light  
>  
> **Story role:** First surface‑world beat after Revival; a quiet chapel hub where the party orients, picks over an abandoned A‑Tech camp, and chooses their first route into the Halt.  
>  
> **When this runs:** Immediately after **Scene B1 — Broken Crypt** once the PCs use Lucifer’s passcode, open the upper door, and step into the ruined chapel interior.  
>  
> **Story job:**  
> - Reveal the **upper chapel** and its view over the **reservoir / dam** in real, moving time.  
> - Show the footprint of an **A‑Tech field camp** that prepared their bodies and then withdrew.  
> - Let the group **loot basic supplies**, heal Injuries, and get their bearings.  
> - Present the **locked storage room** as a small multi‑solution puzzle (keycard, writer, or risky climb).  
> - Have **PAVNN** surface as a low‑key guide with a **datashard briefing** on the **Station** and a nearby **side POI**.  
> - End with the party ready to **leave the chapel** and pick their **first destination**.  
>  
> **Player job:**  
> - Explore the chapel rooms and yard, searching crates, devices, and weird time scars.  
> - Solve access to the **storage / pantry room** using PRY, SYS, or a physical stunt.  
> - Talk with **PAVNN**, ask questions, and decide whether to go straight to the **Station** or detour via the **side cache**.  
>  
> **Tone:**  
> - Quiet, sun‑slanted, hopeful: light through the broken roof, dust motes, faint hum of A‑Tech.  
> - A calm, **trueflow pocket** surrounded by a ruined, mostly frozen world.  
> - Sense of “**second dawn**” plus the distant weight that this is only the very start of a long road.

### Block B2.1 — Up from the Crypt (Entry Room)

> [!question] **TRIGGER — When to run this block**  
> - As soon as they enter the passcode in B1 and open the upper door out of the Broken Crypt.  
> - This covers the **small upstairs room** they emerge into before stepping into the main nave.

> [!abstract] **FEATURES — Entry room**  
> - Narrow, low‑ceilinged **service room** at the top of the stairs.  
> - One **heavy door** back down to the crypt (now mostly flavour).  
> - One door/archway opening into the **main chapel nave**.  
> - A few abandoned **crates and boxes**, some dead cabling and a dark monitor; minor scraps only.  
> - Warm, late‑day light leaks in from the nave; dust hangs in the air.

> [!tip] **GM — Breather & orientation**  
> - Use this as a short **orientation beat** in their new bodies.  
> - Let them poke around for a minute, then naturally draw them into the nave (raised voices, light, etc.).

### Block B2.2 — Main Nave & PAVNN Briefing

> [!abstract] **FEATURES — Chapel nave**  
> - **Ruined sanctuary:** half the roof gone, shattered stone, ivy and moss creeping over pews and pillars.  
> - Through gaps in the walls/roof they see a **reservoir lake** and the distant **dam**, catching late afternoon light.  
> - Clear imprint of a recent **A‑Tech field camp**:  
>   - Bolted‑down **table** with a **card writer** unit and a **note** complaining it’s slow/unreliable.  
>   - Coiled cables, empty supply crates, a couple of dead/idle **A‑Tech drones/android shells** slumped in corners.  
> - On one side wall: the **storage / pantry door** — intact metal, small **digital card reader + text plate**.  
> - Opposite wall: the door that leads back toward the **Broken Crypt stairs** (closed, but no longer plot‑important).  
> - High above, a chunk of **collapsed roof** is frozen mid‑fall, debris held in a distorted pose — a clear **STILLsense hook**.

> [!tip] **GM — Bring PAVNN in gently**  
> - After a few minutes of player exploration, have **PAVNN** come up from the stair or be noticed standing nearby.  
> - He’s low‑key, built for work, voice flat:  
>   - “Directive from Lucifer: brief you on **local transit** and **nearby assets**.”  
> - He plays a short **datashard briefing** (full text in a later pass), which should:  
>   - Identify the **Station** as their primary objective (where the Apocalypse Express waits).  
>   - Flag one nearby **side cache / POI** they *can* hit first for intel/gear but don’t *have* to.  
> - PAVNN answers direct questions succinctly, then mostly listens and follows.

> [!tip] **GM — Silver Postcard state**  
> - Assume the **Silver Postcard** already re‑bound at the end of B1 as agreed.  
> - If you want a quick reminder here, a single line is enough, e.g.:  
>   - “Your pocket prickles with heat; the silver card is still there, patient and heavy.”

### Block B2.3 — Storage Room Puzzle (Hub Overview)

> [!abstract] **GOAL — Open the storage / pantry room**  
> - Door: solid, reinforced, clearly mattered to the A‑Tech crew.  
> - Card reader with **small display** and A‑Tech labelling; no handle on the outside.  
> - Inside: **food, medical supplies, and a few A‑Tech leftovers** (see Loot skeleton below).  
> - Designed as a **low‑stakes puzzle node** with **three main solution paths**:  
>   1. **Find the original keycard** on a dead worker in the **outhouse** outside.  
>   2. **Print a new keycard** using the **card writer + blank cards**.  
>   3. **Climb in from above** through a small broken **high window** (risky physical route).  

> [!tip] **GM — Don’t hard‑lock them**  
> - All three paths are **valid**; players can discover them in any order.  
> - Injuries can happen on harsh failures, but **nobody can die** here and they can **fully heal** in this Stanza.  
> - If they’re spinning wheels, let **PAVNN** nudge toward whichever path they haven’t tried yet.

> [!abstract] **PATH 1 — Outhouse corpse (keycard)**  
> - Small **outhouse/shack** in the yard, wooden door swollen and stuck.  
> - Inside: dead A‑Tech worker who clearly ran here in panic when the apocalypse hit.  
> - The corpse still has a **Storage‑class keycard** on its lanyard.  
> - Door is gated by a **PRY check** (see CHECKS).

> [!abstract] **PATH 2 — Blank cards & card writer**  
> - By the nave writer:  
>   - Small **drawer/locker** with **two blank keycards** and a handwritten note grumbling about the writer being “slow / unreliable”.  
>   - The old **writer unit** itself, scratched and scuffed but powered.  
> - Outside in the yard, a better **replacement writer** sits in/near one of the A‑Tech crates.  
> - **SYS checks** let PCs:  
>   - Read the **door display** to learn it expects **ACCESS: STORAGE**.  
>   - Diagnose the **old writer** as glitchy but functional.  
>   - Use either writer to **program a Storage keycard**, with better odds on the newer unit.

> [!abstract] **PATH 3 — Climb & drop (roof window)**  
> - Exterior chapel wall has **jutting stones and broken ledges** up to a small **high window** into the storage room.  
> - Clearly **risky** at a glance: loose stone, bad angle, hard floor inside.  
> - Requires a **climb check**, then a **finesse/tinkering check** on the window, then a **landing check** on the drop (see CHECKS).  
> - GM should **telegraph danger** up front: this is the “bold but hazardous” route.

### Block B2.4 — Skill Showcases & Checks

> [!warning] **RULE — System ON (limited)**  
> - From this scene on, **normal checks** apply (AE + 5e bridge).  
> - **Chaos / CDR / Harmony** remain **OFF** until the Dam sequence.  
> - **Revival printing** is not yet available; Injuries here matter, but this Stanza offers **enough tools to fix most of them**.

> [!abstract] **CHECKS — Key gates (AE first; 5e bridge in parentheses)**  
> Use these as defaults; you can move DCs up/down if play demands, but keep the **bands** and **consequences** intact.

**1. Outhouse door (PRY to force Path 1)**  
- **AE:** PRY vs **DC 12** (routine).  
- **5e:** STR (Athletics) vs DC 12.  

**Outcomes:**  
- **CF (≤ DC−5):** Door bursts inward and the PC **tumbles into the pit**. No HP loss, but gain **1 Minor Injury** (twisted knee, foul inhalation, etc.). They still reach the interior and can find the corpse + card.  
- **F (DC−4 to DC−1):** Door booms loudly but stays shut. They can **try again**, but the noise is unsettling.  
- **S (DC to DC+4):** Door forces open with effort; inside is disgusting but navigable. Corpse + keycard are found with a brief search.  
- **CS (≥ DC+5):** As Success, plus the PC spots an extra **log scrap** hinting at the worker’s panic and linking to the wider apocalypse.

---

**2. Storage door — SYS read (identify “Storage”)**  
- **AE:** SYS vs **DC 12** to read; **DC 15** for deeper menu access.  
- **5e:** INT (Investigation) or INT (Arcana/Tech) vs same DCs.  

**Outcomes:**  
- **F or worse:** Only “ACCESS DENIED — CARD REQUIRED” shows. They know they need a card, nothing else.  
- **S (DC 12):** Display clearly labels the door as **ACCESS CLASS: STORAGE**; implies other classes exist.  
- **CS (DC 15+):** As Success, plus a small log line like “Last valid access: STORAGE ×3”, hinting that **three cards** existed.

---

**3. Card writer — diagnose & use (Path 2)**  

*Old writer (in nave)*  
- **AE:** SYS vs **DC 12** to understand; **DC 15** to safely program.  
- **5e:** INT (Tinker/Tools) vs same.  

*New writer (yard crate)*  
- **AE:** SYS vs **DC 10** to confirm it’s good; **DC 12** to program.  
- **5e:** As above with slightly lower DCs.  

*Programming a blank card:*  
- **CF:** Card becomes a useless misprint; mark **1 blank consumed**. Device reports an error.  
- **F:** Error; card remains clearly invalid but not consumed.  
- **S:** Card successfully imprinted as **Storage**, assuming they picked the correct access type from the menu.  
- **CS:** As Success; optionally grant that PC **Soft Advantage on one future SYS check** in this Stanza (they “get” A‑Tech UI better now).

> [!tip] **GM — Access type clue**  
> - Put the list of access types where they can read it (door plate or writer UI): STORAGE / OPS / LAB / ADMIN, etc.  
> - The **SYS door read** is the cleanest way to learn “Storage” is correct.

---

**4. Storage door — brute force PRY (alternate)**  
- **AE:** PRY vs **DC 18** (hard).  
- **5e:** STR (Athletics) vs DC 18.  

**Outcomes:**  
- **CF:** Door finally gives, but the effort plus falling stone causes **1 Major Injury**. Storage opens; lock is ruined.  
- **F:** Door doesn’t open and the strain causes **1 Minor Injury** (pulled shoulder, bruised ribs).  
- **S / CS:** Door is forced open; lock destroyed, storage accessible.

---

**5. Climb & drop path (Path 3)**  

*Climb exterior wall*  
- **AE:** RES vs **DC 15**.  
- **5e:** STR (Athletics) or DEX (Acrobatics) vs DC 15.  

*Force high window*  
- **AE:** SYS or PRY vs **DC 12**.  
- **5e:** DEX (Sleight of Hand) or STR (Athletics) vs DC 12.  

*Drop down inside*  
- **AE:** RES vs **DC 12**.  
- **5e:** DEX (Acrobatics) vs DC 12.  

*Drop outcomes:*  
- **CF:** PC takes **1 Major Injury** from a bad landing.  
- **F:** PC takes **1 Minor Injury**.  
- **S / CS:** They land well and are inside the storage room; door can later be opened from within.

> [!warning] **Safety — Injuries only**  
> - In B2, **never combine these with HP damage**.  
> - Consequences are **Minor/Major Injuries + narrative discomfort**, and the storage room contains enough healing to clear most of them.

---

**6. STILLsense & GODSight showcases**  

- **STILLsense — Frozen roof segment**  
  - **AE:** STILLsense vs **DC 12**.  
  - **On Success:** PC feels the roof as a **knife‑edge between Halt and Trueflow**. Deliver a short codex‑ish line about sacred places distorting the Halt.  
  - **On Crit:** Add a hint why this chapel is relatively stable (old warding, latent faith, Lucifer’s adjustments, etc.).  

- **GODSight — Outhouse / altar vision**  
  - **AE:** GODSight vs **DC 12** at the outhouse or surviving altar.  
  - **On Success:** Flash of the **worker’s last moments** — angels/demons descending, sky tearing, earth splitting; the worker runs for the outhouse, keycard visible on their chest.  
  - **On Crit:** Vision zooms out to show the **dual apocalypse** in full: man‑made ruin and divine purge overlaid, echoing Lucifer’s earlier description.

> [!tip] **GM — Awareness (AWS)**  
> - Treat **AWS** mostly as **passive** here.  
> - PCs with strong Awareness get:  
>   - Early spot on the **better writer** in an outside crate.  
>   - A nudge toward the **crowbar** (PRY helper) in the yard.  
>   - First sense that the **frozen roof** is more than just rubble, justifying a STILLsense roll.

### Block B2.5 — Storage Room Interior & Loot Skeleton

> [!abstract] **FEATURES — Inside the storage / pantry**  
> - Shelving with **sealed food packs, water, and basic supplies** for a small field team.  
> - A simple **workbench** with a few repair tools and a half‑finished A‑Tech device.  
> - One or more **medical cases** with neatly organised kits.  
> - 1–2 **datashards / paper logs** tying this team to the **Station** and the **side POI** PAVNN mentions.

> [!abstract] **LOOT PROFILE — Chapel storage (skeleton)**  
> Use **Loot Categories & Materials** for final itemisation; this block fixes the **shape**, not the exact items.  
>  
> - **Medical & Chems:**  
>   - Enough to **treat ~3–4 Injuries** (e.g., several basic medkits, bandages, etc.).  
> - **Survival & Logistics:**  
>   - A handful of **food / water items** that can also patch up HP as per your healing rules.  
> - **Tools & Tech:**  
>   - 1× **crowbar‑equivalent** (tagged as a tool that also boosts PRY fictionally).  
>   - Optional small tool kit if desired (field repair kit, multitool, etc.).  
> - **Keys, Intel & Boons:**  
>   - 1–2 logs / shards that:  
>     - Reinforce the **Station** as goal.  
>     - Sketch the **side POI** as an option (threat level, reward type, etc.).

> [!tip] **GM — Healing expectation**  
> - The intent is that a careful party can leave B2 with:  
>   - **Most starting Injuries cured**,  
>   - A few **healing items in reserve**, and  
>   - One or two small **utility tools**,  
>   without feeling over‑loaded.
