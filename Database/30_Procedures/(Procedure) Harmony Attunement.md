# 🎼 Harmony Attunement
^top

> [!summary]+ 🧭 On this page
> [[#^tldr|TL;DR]] · [[#^effect|Effect]] · [[#^limits|Limits & Interactions]] · [[#^beltline|Roll‑State Fit]] · [[#^order|Order of Operations]] · [[#^examples|Examples]] · [[#^accept|✅ Acceptance checks]] · [[#^dm|DM Notes]] · [[#^changes|🔄 Changelog]]

> [!tldr] 💎 **TL;DR**
> A **license**, not a slot: you can attune to items that **require Harmony Attunement**. You still spend a **short rest**, it uses **one normal attunement slot**, and you must meet **all other item prerequisites**.

---

## 🔑 Effect
^effect

When you possess **Harmony Attunement**, you can **attune** to magic, unique, A‑Tech etc.  items that include the requirement **“Requires: Harmony Attunement.”**

- **Short rest required.** Attuning takes a **short rest** with the item in your possession.
- **Consumes a normal slot.** Attunement **uses one** of your normal attunement slots; this talent **does not increase** your limit.
- **Other gates still apply.** You must meet all **other prerequisites** listed on the item (class, level, spellcasting, pact magic, lineage/background, narrative flags, etc.).
- **Multiple items allowed.** You may be attuned to **multiple Harmony‑tagged items at once**, limited by your **attunement slots** and each item’s additional gates.

---

## ⚖️ Limits & Interactions
^limits

- **License, not a slot.** This talent **never** grants extra attunement capacity.
- **No bypasses.** It satisfies only the “Harmony Attunement” requirement; it **does not** satisfy **Chaos Attunement** (if present) or any other gate.
- **Loss & suppression.** If you lose this talent (curse, rewrite), items that require it become **suppressed** (properties dormant) until you regain the talent. Suppressed items **still occupy a slot** until you end attunement. Reattuning later requires a new **short rest**.
- **Once per character.** You may **choose this talent only once** per character (available at HL **4/6/8** minor tiers).
- **Rest windows created by Chaos.** **Instant short/long rest** effects (e.g., **Chaos Table rows 19/22**) **do not allow** attuning/unattuning because those rows **explicitly forbid activities requiring the rest’s duration**.
- **Printing & rests.** Revival **printing isn’t a rest**; it does **not** allow attuning. Finish a real **short rest** afterward if you wish to attune.
- **Site & timing.** Attune only when conditions allow a short rest (safe, uninterrupted). Harmony Unlocks that grant **short‑rest services** still require the full timing.

## 🧵 Roll‑State Fit (beltline)
^beltline

> **Invariant pipeline:** **Gate → Hard Override → Soft Override → d20 → Offsets → Bands.**  
> **Harmony Attunement** never alters this order, doesn’t grant advantage, and adds no Offsets by itself.

---

## 🧭 Order of Operations (attune/suppress/reattune)
^order

1) **Check eligibility:** You have this talent **and** meet all other item prerequisites.  
2) **Attune:** Spend a **short rest**; the item uses **one attunement slot**.  
3) **Lose license (if it happens):** The item is **suppressed** (properties dormant) but continues to occupy a slot until you end attunement.  
4) **Regain license:** **Reattune** with a **short rest** to reactivate the item.

---

## 🧪 Examples
^examples

- **Keyed blade.** You find a sword that reads *Requires: Harmony Attunement*. With this talent, you spend a **short rest** and attune; it uses **one slot**.  
- **No bypass.** A bracer reads *Requires: Harmony Attunement, Pact Magic*. You **cannot** attune unless you also have **Pact Magic**.  
- **Suppressed item.** A curse strips your license. Your Harmony‑licensed cloak goes **dormant** but **still holds a slot** until you end attunement.  
- **Chaos row 19.** You gain an **instant short rest** from the Chaos table. You **cannot** use that window to attune because the effect **forbids** activities that require the rest’s duration.

## ✅ Acceptance checks
^accept

- Taking this talent **does not** add attunement slots.  
- You **can** attune items that say **“Requires: Harmony Attunement.”**  
- Items with **additional gates** (e.g., “requires spellcasting”) still **require** those gates.  
- **Instant short/long rest** Chaos results **do not permit** attuning/unattuning.  
- If you **lose** the talent, Harmony‑licensed items are **suppressed** and **still occupy a slot** until you end attunement.  
- **Printing isn’t a rest**; you cannot attune during revival printing.

## 🛠️ DM Notes (optional)
^dm

- **Design intent.** A **gating key** for high‑impact Harmony gear—**access**, not raw numbers.  
- **Content floor.** To avoid a “dead pick,” ensure the party encounters Harmony‑licensed items on a steady cadence: **≥1 by HL6**, **≥2 by HL8** (party‑wide).  
- **Fairness knob.** If a character takes this and sees **no** Harmony‑licensed items by the **end of the next chapter**, you may designate **one** suitable mid‑tier item as **license‑free for that chapter’s drop**, or allow a **one‑time swap** at the **next level‑up**.
