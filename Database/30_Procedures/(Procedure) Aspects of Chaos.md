# 🔥 Aspects of Chaos — **Lucifer’s Mark** (Ritual)

> [!tldr] 💎 **TL;DR — The one‑line idea**
> **Learn a personal Verse → at a Marking Station, read your 4 lines while a Witness arms the pour → you absorb the mark and gain **+1 Chaos Level (CL)** (cap 10).** One benefit **per chapter per character**. Verse is **consumed only on success**.

^concept
## Concept — rare **Marking Station** that raises **CL**
**Lucifer’s Mark** is a **scene‑gated ritual**. Each **eligible** character who completes it gains **+1 Chaos Level (CL)** immediately (to a maximum of **CL 10**). It **does not** change **Harmony Level (HL)**, currencies, or any other meters.

> [!tip] 🎚️ Why it matters
> A higher **CL** shifts your **Chaos dice** on future **Chaos Rolls** (wider range; bigger swings). The ritual doesn’t grant any other power by itself.

---

^eligibility
## ✅ Eligibility & Requirements
To benefit, a character must meet **both** conditions:
1) **Verse (unspent).** You **know** a **personal 4‑line Verse** for Lucifer’s Mark (earned earlier as a reward, clue, quest boon, etc.).  
2) **Station present.** You are physically at a **Marking Station** (the altar/basin/control plate).

**Ineligible press:** If you **lack** a Verse, the shale **stays matte**; no verse appears; nothing happens.

> [!warning] 🧷 Personal, not pooled
> Verses are **personal knowledge**. They aren’t currency and can’t be sold. The table may allow **gifting at award time** if the scene says so; otherwise **no transfers**.

---

^setup
## 🧰 Setup (constant props)
- **Control** with printed activation phrase: **“Dark tide, wake.”**
- **Shale tablet**: a finger **press** reveals a Verse if you have one. The “cut” is **fictional**; no damage.
- **Basin/zone**: the dark medium **repels all** until the pour; only marked readers absorb.

> [!note] 👁️ Roles
> - **Recipients (many):** any number of **eligible** PCs.  
> - **Witness (1):** a consenting PC at the controls. If none, an **NPC** or **PAVNN** can serve so play never stalls.  
> - *(Optional)* **Choir:** bystanders may echo for flavor; no mechanical effect.

---

^procedure
## ⚙️ Procedure — **five clear steps**
1) **Window opens.** GM frames the Station; invite **eligible** Recipients to step into the basin (still repelled).  
2) **Press & Verse.** One by one, each Recipient **presses** the shale → their **personal 4 lines** appear if they hold a Verse → they **read all 4** (≈ **8–12 seconds**). A brief **personal sign** (1–2s) flickers; repulsion remains.  
3) **Arm.** The **Witness** performs a visible action at the control and says **“Dark tide, wake.”**  
4) **Pour.** The black liquid **descends** and **absorbs only into those who read**; others remain repelled.  
5) **Trace.** Record **Marked / Witness** on the site’s board (**social memory only**; **not currency**).

---

^effects
## 🎯 What changes on success — **+1 Chaos Level (CL)**
- Increase your **CL by 1** **immediately** (max **10**). This affects **future** Chaos Rolls (the **dice & offset** for your level).  
- This ritual **never** lowers **HL** or alters your **Seals**, **Devil’s Mercy** limits, **IAZ/ASH**, **CDR**, or **currencies**.

> [!tip] 📈 Chaos Level ladder (quick feel)
> **CL1** skews to rows **9–15** (small swings). **CL10** reaches **2–22** (largest swings). Use your table’s **Master Chaos Table** for exact dice per level.

> [!warning] ⛔ At cap
> At **CL 10**, you **can’t** go higher. If you attempt the ritual at cap, you get **Trace only**; your **Verse is not consumed**.

---

^cadence
## 🗓️ Cadence & caps
- **Per character:** You can **benefit at most once per chapter**. Further attempts this chapter create **Trace only**; your **Verse is not consumed**.  
- **Multiple recipients:** Any number of **eligible** PCs may mark in the **same window**; each gains **+1 CL** and **consumes one Verse** on success.  
- **Station rarity:** Marking Stations are **rare**; scenes place them as milestones, vaults, or sancta.

> [!tip] 📝 Verse consumption
> Your **Verse** is **consumed** only on a **successful absorption** (the pour step) that increases your **CL**. Otherwise it **remains** with you.

---

^verse
## ✍️ Verse rules (personal; revealed on contact)
- **Form:** **4 lines**, each **6–8 syllables**.  
- **Rhyme:** **AABB** or **ABAB** (**perfect** end‑rhymes).  
- **Diction:** clear, speakable; no tongue‑twisters.  
- **Timing:** read in **8–12 seconds**.  
- **Reader:** only the **Recipient** reads (the Witness never reads).  
- **Fallback if unprepared:** use one stock couplet + two deed‑specific lines (keep meter/rhyme).

---

^interactions
## ⚖️ Interactions & **non‑effects** (guardrails)
- **Harmony:** No change to **HL**, **Seals**, or **HU** access.  
- **Devil’s Mercy:** No change to **per‑holder** or **party** limits.  
- **IAZ/ASH:** The ritual **isn’t** an ASH; it doesn’t begin/end an IAZ.  
- **Economy:** **Trace** and Verses are **not** **LEV/PLUG/ST** and **cannot** be sold.  
- **Beltline fit:** The ritual **doesn’t** alter roll‑state math; it only changes your **future CL**.

---

^accept
## ✅ Acceptance checks (adversarial quick‑tests)
- **CL gain:** A PC at **CL 4** with a Verse completes the ritual → **CL 5** immediately. Future Chaos Rolls use **CL 5** dice.  
- **Same chapter, second try:** The same PC returns later with another Verse in the **same chapter** → **Trace only**; **CL** unchanged; **Verse not consumed**.  
- **At cap:** A PC at **CL 10** completes the ritual → **no effect**; **Verse not consumed**; **Trace** recorded.  
- **Group window:** Two PCs with Verses read in the same window; both gain **+1 CL**; the **Witness** gains **no change**.  
- **No Verse:** A PC without a Verse presses the shale → **no text appears**; they **cannot** absorb; **no effect**.

---

^examples
## 🧪 Examples
- **Cache reward → later station:** You found **two Verses** in a cache. Weeks later you reach a Station: you and the scout each mark once (both **+1 CL**). You keep **one Verse** for later.  
- **Repeat within the chapter:** You marked this chapter already. A second Station appears: your slate shows your Verse, but the pour results only in **Trace**; you save the Verse for next chapter.
