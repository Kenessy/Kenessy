# Apocalypse Express — Sora Base Scenes (v0.1)

> These **base scene prompts** stay **static**. You will append **style modules** beneath them later (e.g., Cinematic Hyperreal, Painterly‑Cinematic, Arcane Technical Noir). Keep anchor nouns the same across runs to reduce drift.

## How to run (Sora 2 API settings)
- **model:** `sora-2` (or `sora-2-pro` if available)  
- **seconds:** `8` (use 4 or 12 as variants)  
- **size:** `1280x720` (or `1792x1024` on sora-2-pro)  
- Keep **one camera move** + **one subject action** per shot. Favor short clips and stitch if needed.

## Scene 01 — The Skybridge Run (DO NOT CHANGE)

**SCENE PURPOSE**: Wide exterior establishing shot that tests landscape scale, time freeze, and the moving train vs frozen world.

**SUBJECT**: A heavy, oily‑black steam locomotive (“The Express”), riveted dark‑steel plates, rectangular smokebox door, single central headlight, laddered tender; furnace mouth intermittently visible through narrow slats.

**ENVIRONMENT**: High stone viaduct over a deep river gorge. In middle background: a town with one building halted mid‑collapse; glass shards and torn posters suspended in the air; a fork of lightning **frozen** in mid‑strike near a distant substation.

**TIME & LIGHT**: Late‑afternoon sun; thin **purple chronon haze** in uneven veils; light ash/dust in the air (frozen).

**PHYSICS RULE**: Everything in the world is perfectly frozen **except** the train and its steam/smoke.

**CAMERA**: 50 mm lens, eye‑level to slightly low. **Movement**: slow lateral dolly or locked‑off. Framing: rule of thirds; train nose crosses the right third; clear fore/mid/background layers.

**MOTION**: The train advances at a steady, believable speed. Steam and smoke behave naturally. **No other motion** (leaves, paper, lightning remain frozen).

**COMPOSITION**: One clear focal point (train nose). Leading lines from the viaduct and rails. Background stays subordinate and low contrast.

**PALETTE CONSTRAINTS**: Basalt neutrals for world; **brand violet** in haze/shadows; **warm gold rim** on steel; **ember** restricted to furnace slits only. **Cyan** reserved for diegetic info signs if present (optional).

**NEGATIVE**: no neon, no cartoon outlines, no elves/dwarves, no whimsical steampunk gear clutter, no cyberpunk holograms, no rainbow lighting.

## Scene 02 — The Eye in the Maintenance Chapel (DO NOT CHANGE)

**SCENE PURPOSE**: Controlled interior with sacred‑tech object; tests close‑to‑mid framing, atmosphere, and precision overlays.

**SUBJECT**: **The Eye of God** — a matte‑black spherical core (heavy, simple form) with **fine gold tracery lines** and a thin **rotating outer ring**. The object **levitates 30 cm** above a basalt pedestal.

**ENVIRONMENT**: Decommissioned maintenance chapel: dark steel ribs, basalt floor slabs, tool alcoves, a narrow catwalk overhead. Dust motes hang motionless.

**TIME & LIGHT**: Low‑key interior lit by a single warm overhead practical (golden‑hour color), plus faint violet ambient from the doors.

**PHYSICS RULE**: The world is frozen; **only the Eye’s outer ring rotates slowly**.

**CAMERA**: 85 mm lens, **slow push‑in**; minimal parallax; Eye centered. Shallow DoF isolates the Eye but keeps pedestal readable.

**OVERLAYS (DIEGETIC)**: Thin **cyan** measurement lines/labels (≤30% opacity) skim over the Eye for ~1–2 s, then fade. They must feel **in‑world**, not big UI panels.

**PALETTE CONSTRAINTS**: Black/charcoal materials; brand violet ambient; **precise gold** on the Eye only; cyan overlays only as measurement; **no broad cyan washes**; **no ember** unless a hazard is explicitly shown.

**NEGATIVE**: no ornate gothic overload; no neon; no cartoon rim lights; no hologram billboards; no runes everywhere.

## Scene 03 — Lucifer on the Platform (DO NOT CHANGE)

**SCENE PURPOSE**: Character portrait in world context; tests human realism, wardrobe, and controlled background motion.

**SUBJECT**: **Lucifer** — middle‑aged human male, black hair, clean‑shaven, tailored **black suit** and a single **red tie** (matte fabric). Hands in coat pockets; neutral expression.

**ENVIRONMENT**: Outdoor rail platform with cast‑iron lamps and weathered signage. Stone station wall with timetable behind glass.

**TIME & LIGHT**: Late‑afternoon; soft **gold** key from camera‑left; cool **violet** ambient in shadows; faint purple haze over tracks.

**PHYSICS RULE**: Platform crowd, papers, raindrops, and lamps are **frozen** in place. **Only the train in the background moves** past slowly; furnace flicker visible.

**CAMERA**: 100–120 mm lens, waist‑up framing, eye‑level; subtle **slow push‑in**; background blur separates Lucifer from the train.

**MOTION**: Train passes behind at believable speed; steam/smoke behave naturally; Lucifer remains still.

**PALETTE CONSTRAINTS**: Basalt neutrals for world; **gold light** on edges; **violet** in shadows/haze; **single red accent** limited to the tie. No other red elements.

**NEGATIVE**: no demonic mutations, no horns, no glowing eyes, no neon casts, no cyberpunk signage, no cartoon outlines.
