# Section 0 — Null Meridian Prelude

> [!summary] **SCENE A1 — Waking in the Null Meridian**  
> TYPE: Talk, Explore  
> LENGTH: Medium  
> DANGER: Safe  
> PURPOSE: Intro  
> TEACHING: Tutorial  
>  
> **When this runs:** First playable moment of the campaign, immediately after “you died / there was nothing,” and **before** any rules, stats, or character options are mentioned.  
>  
> **Story job:**  
> - Show that the PCs are **dead souls in a between‑place**, not yet in bodies.  
> - Establish the **Null Meridian** as a calm, wrong “waiting room” outside normal physics.  
> - Let the group realise they are not alone here.  
>  
> **Player job:**  
> - Say how their soul feels **right now** (emotions, impressions, shape hints).  
> - Poke at the environment to discover what does / doesn’t respond.  
> - Start reacting to each other as strangers on the same impossible platform.  
>  
> **Tone:**  
> - Quiet, uncanny, liminal; eerie but not hostile.  
> - Emphasise stillness, looping space, and the sense that **something is observing / scheduling** them.

> [!danger] **RULE — System OFF in this scene**  
> - No HP, damage, skills, saves, spells, Chaos, CDR, Harmony, currencies, or Injuries apply here.  
> - Class features and items **do not function** mechanically; any “effect” is cosmetic only.  
> - Nothing in this scene can kill or injure them “again” or spend resources.  
> - Any dice you roll are **flavour only** (e.g., what kind of emotion leaks through), never success vs failure.

---

### Block A1.1 — Wake‑Up (Trigger & Read‑Aloud)

> [!question] **TRIGGER — When to run this block**  
> - Use this at the very start of Session 1, after you’ve framed that the characters died / there was nothing.  
> - Run **before** character sheets, classes, or any mechanical explanation.  
> - After this block, move into free play on the platform using A1.2 and A1.3.

> [!quote] **READ ALOUD — Wake on the Null Meridian**  
> Cold nothing gives way to stone under your hands.  
>  
> A circular platform hangs in open dark, its edge traced by a dull red glow. Around you, a few blurred shapes shudder and drag themselves upright, bodies half‑formed, as if something is still deciding what you’re all supposed to be.

---

### Block A1.2 — Senses on the Platform

> [!tip] **GM — How to use this block**  
> - Right after the read‑aloud, ask: **“What’s the first thing you do or notice?”**  
> - When players ask “what do I see / hear / feel?”, answer from these lists.  
> - Don’t dump everything at once; use this as a **lookup table** while you play.

> [!abstract] **SENSES — Quick answers**

**SIGHT**

- A rough stone or concrete **platform** with no railings, floating in featureless black.  
- A thin, steady **red line** traces the rim; it doesn’t quite behave like light or metal.  
- Each PC appears as a **shifting shadow‑body**: mostly humanoid, edges blurring and snapping between possible shapes.  
- Any other silhouettes stay dim and inert unless you decide to “promote” one later (extra PCs/NPCs).

**SOUND**

- Movements are slightly **muffled**, like a padded room or heavy fog.  
- Voices carry **too clearly**; words echo back faintly from no direction you can point to.  
- Underneath is a slow, distant **mechanical rhythm** — a clack / heartbeat / engine that hasn’t yet resolved into anything specific.

**FEEL**

- Gravity feels **normal**; they have balance and weight, but no breath, heartbeat, or skin sensation.  
- The floor is solid and cool under hand and foot.  
- Reaching toward the platform’s rim or out into the dark feels subtly **wrong**, as if there’s a glass wall ahead even before they touch the edge.

---

### Block A1.3 — Player Actions & Null Physics

> [!tip] **GM — What this block is for**  
> Use this once players start actively **doing things** beyond just looking around.  
> It tells you how the Null behaves when they:  
> - inspect their own form  
> - go to the edge / try to leave  
> - attempt violence or self‑harm  
> - call out or pray  
> - dig for memories  
> - try spells or class features  
> - scan the void for more detail  

> [!abstract] **CORE NULL RULES — Always true here**  
> - You **cannot leave** the platform. Space loops; the void never takes you.  
> - You **cannot hurt** anything here — no damage, no marks, no HP, no Injuries.  
> - Everyone is a **half‑formed soul**, not yet in a proper body.  
> - **Past‑life details are smudged**; only emotional tones bleed through.  
> - Time feels **stalled / supervised**, not natural.  
> - **No real rolls**: any dice are flavour only, never success vs failure.

---

#### 3.1 “What do I look like?”

> [!abstract] **FEATURE — Testing their own form**  

**Player move:** “I look at myself / my hands / my body,” or studies another soul.

**Describe (pick 1–2):**

- Their outline **flickers** between 2–3 versions (baseline, idealised, monstrous, symbolic).  
- When they focus on “what I want to be,” the form **leans that way** for a heartbeat, then blurs back to a generic shadow.  
- Touching another soul feels like **cold, thick air**: resistance without skin, no true contact.

**Table takeaway:** They are **souls, not finished bodies**; identity is still in flux.

---

#### 3.2 “I go to the edge / I jump / I walk away”

> [!abstract] **FEATURE — Testing the edge**  

**Player move:** walks to the rim, jumps, or strides off into the dark.

**Describe (pick 1–2):**

- Each step toward the edge feels like pushing through **invisible resistance**; the rim seems both near and far.  
- If they step off or jump: a single **lurch of falling** into cold dark, then their foot hits stone again **where they started**.  
- From others’ perspective, they simply **blink** from edge to centre; there’s no visible fall.

**Table takeaway:** They **cannot leave**; space is looped around the platform.

---

#### 3.3 “I hit it / stab / break / kill”

> [!abstract] **FEATURE — Trying violence or self‑harm**  

**Player move:** attacks self, another soul, or the platform; tries to break or kill something.

**Describe (pick 1–2):**

- Limbs or weapons **pass through** like smoke, or connect with **no pain and no mark**.  
- Reality **skips a frame**: a brief tear in the scene, then everything snaps back untouched.  
- The target may feel a **cold tug** or lurch of intent, but nothing actually changes.

**Table takeaway:** Here, **nothing can be damaged or killed**. They’re already as dead as they’re going to get.

---

#### 3.4 “Hello? Is anyone out there?” / praying / cursing

> [!abstract] **FEATURE — Calling out**  

**Player move:** shouts into the void, prays, curses, or calls for help; or speaks to other silhouettes.

**Describe (pick 1–2):**

- Words aimed at the dark come back as a **soft, delayed echo** from no direction.  
- The sound is **too clean**; every syllable rings in the emptiness.  
- After a few calls, the distant **mechanical rhythm** feels like it tightens in response, but never answers in words.  
- When they address other souls directly, voices sound **normal**; conversation between them is real.

**Table takeaway:** Contact with the void is **one‑way**; contact with each other is solid.

---

#### 3.5 “What do I remember?”

> [!abstract] **FEATURE — Probing memory**  

**Player move:** asks what they remember of their life, their death, or who they were.

**Describe (pick 1–2):**

- Names, faces, and concrete events are **fogged out**, like text rubbed off a page.  
- They get a strong **emotional residue** from their end (anger, guilt, duty, relief, etc.).  
- They may sense *how* they died in broad strokes (“violence”, “sickness”, “sacrifice”) but never as a clear scene.

**Optional prompt back:**

- “At the end, you remember feeling mostly **[angry / exhausted / resigned / hopeful]**. Which one feels right?”

**Table takeaway:** Past‑life detail is locked; this beat is about **present emotion**, not biography.

---

#### 3.6 “I cast a spell / rage / smite / shapeshift”

> [!abstract] **FEATURE — Trying class features**  

**Player move:** attempts to cast, rage, smite, shapeshift, etc.

**Describe (pick 1–2):**

- Power **starts to gather** — words, sigils, muscle memory — then fizzles as if the scene won’t support it.  
- A faint outline of the effect appears for a heartbeat (glow, sparks, spectral weapon), then collapses back into their shadow.  
- There’s no cost, no backlash, no resource spent — just the sense that **whatever fuels that power isn’t connected here**.

**Table takeaway:** Class kits are **dormant** in the Null; this is before sheets, not a “low‑magic zone.”

---

#### 3.7 “I look/listen harder” / scanning the void

> [!abstract] **FEATURE — Scanning the environment**  

**Player move:** carefully observes the darkness or stands still to “sense” what’s out there.

**Describe (pick 1–2):**

- The void stays mostly blank; on a long stare they **almost** see twin red lines in the distance like rails, then lose them when they blink.  
- The slow mechanical rhythm sharpens just enough to feel like **something counting or turning** out there.  
- Time feels off: they can’t tell whether seconds or minutes are passing.

**Table takeaway:** There is **structure out there** (the Line / Train), but it’s not reachable yet.
