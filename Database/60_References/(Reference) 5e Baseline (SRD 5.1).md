What this note is:
 concise, player‑facing summary of **core D&D 5e** mechanics per the **SRD 5.1**. It explains how play works at the table without reproducing non‑SRD book text.

---

## Core Principles
- **d20 System:** Roll **1d20 + modifiers** against a target number (**DC** or **AC**). Meet or exceed to succeed.
- **Modifiers:** Add your **ability modifier** and, if you're trained, your **proficiency bonus**.
- **Advantage/Disadvantage:** Roll **2d20**, take the **higher** (advantage) or **lower** (disadvantage). Multiple sources don’t stack; if you have both, they cancel.
- **Bounded Accuracy:** Numbers stay small; a +1 to +3 matters a lot.

---

## Ability Scores & Proficiency
- **Scores & Mods:** STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA. Modifier = **floor((score − 10)/2)**.  
  *Examples:* 10→+0, 12→+1, 14→+2, 18→+4, 20→+5.
- **Proficiency Bonus (PB):** +2 at level 1; then **+3 (5th), +4 (9th), +5 (13th), +6 (17th)**.
- **Skills & Tools:** If proficient, add PB to relevant **ability checks**. Some checks are “**passive**” at **10 + all modifiers**.

---

## Checks, Saves, and Initiative
- **Ability Check:** d20 + ability mod (+ PB if proficient) vs **DC**.
- **Saving Throw:** d20 + listed save mod vs **save DC** to resist an effect.
- **Initiative:** d20 + DEX mod to set turn order when combat starts.

---

## Combat — Turn & Action Economy
- **Round & Turn:** A round ≈ **6 seconds**. On **your turn**:  
  - **Move** up to your speed.  
  - Take **1 Action** (Attack, Cast a Spell, Dash, Disengage, Dodge, Help, Hide, Ready, Search, Use an Object).  
  - Possibly **1 Bonus Action** (only when something grants it).  
  - **1 Reaction** between turns (e.g., opportunity attack).  
  - **Free Interactions** for simple things (speak, draw/stow, open door, etc.).
- **Attack Roll:** d20 + attack modifiers vs target **AC**. On a **natural 20**, it’s a **critical hit**: roll all damage **dice twice** and add modifiers once.
- **Damage:** Apply resistance (**half**) or vulnerability (**double**) if present.
- **Cover:** **Half** cover = **+2** to AC and DEX saves; **Three‑quarters** = **+5**; **Total cover** can’t be targeted.
- **Movement:** You can split movement before/after your action. **Difficult terrain** costs double. Leaving an enemy’s reach usually provokes a **reaction** attack.

---

## Spellcasting — Basics
- **Spell Save DC:** **8 + PB + casting ability mod**.  
- **Spell Attack Modifier:** **PB + casting ability mod**.  
- **Components & Time:** A spell needs the listed **components** (V/S/M) and **casting time** to work.  
- **Concentration:** Only **one** concentration spell at a time; dropping to **0 HP**, becoming **incapacitated**, or **casting another concentration spell** ends it. When you take damage, make a **CON save**: **DC 10** or **half the damage (round down)**, whichever is higher.

---

## Resting & Healing (Default)
- **Short Rest (~1 hour):** Spend **Hit Dice (HD)** to heal: roll HD, add CON mod per die spent.  
- **Long Rest (~8 hours):** Restore **all HP**, and regain up to **half your maximum HD** (round down), plus class features that refresh on a long rest.

---

## Dropping to 0 HP, Death Saves
- At **0 HP**, you fall **unconscious** and start making **Death Saving Throws** at the start of your turns: **d20** (no mods).  
  - **3 successes** → **stable** (0 HP, unconscious).  
  - **3 failures** → **dead**.  
  - **Natural 20** → you regain **1 HP**.  
  - **Natural 1** → counts as **two failures**.  
  - Taking damage while at 0 HP usually adds a failed save (a **critical hit** counts as **two**).

---

## Conditions (Common)
**Blinded, Charmed, Deafened, Frightened, Grappled, Incapacitated, Invisible, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained, Stunned, Unconscious, Exhaustion.**  
Each condition has specific effects that apply until it ends.

---

## Encumbrance & Equipment (Default)
- **Carrying Capacity:** Typically **15 × STR score** (in pounds).  
- **Armor:** Affects **AC**; some armors impose **stealth disadvantage**; **shield = +2 AC**.  
- **Weapons:** Melee often use **STR**; ranged and **finesse** can use **DEX**.

---

## Common Target Numbers
Typical DCs appear across play: **5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30**. Set by the situation and adjudicated at the table.
