# Revival — Lucifer & Quantum Print
^top

> [!tldr] 💎 **Player TL;DR**
> **Die?** The Train prints a **new body**.  
> **Cost (per character):** **Sparkplugs = your Soul Debt (SD) at Start**. **Start at SD 1**. **Accrual:** Core **+1** per decant; Hardcore **+1/+2/+3** by Majors at death (0–1/2/3).
> **Modes:**  
> • 🔌 **Sparkplug Print** — **8 h** · Train **normal ops** · **parallel** (per bay) · **1d4−1 Minor** injury at decant.  
> • 🔋 **Reserve Print** — **8 h + 2d4** · Train **Low‑Energy (locked)** · **sequential 1×1** · **+1 Major** injury at decant.  
> **Mixing:** if **any** active print is **Reserve**, the Train is **Low‑Energy** & **sequential**; you **can’t** start Sparkplug prints until **no** Reserve is active.  
> **Time:** clocks tick; **printing isn’t a rest** (others may rest; the printed body does not).  
> **⚡ Hellstatic (HS):** Only **at 3 Major injuries**. Before each **declared** test, roll **HS d20**; if **≤ effective HS**, your test is at **HARD Disadvantage** (**HARD overrides SOFT; HARD vs HARD = Normal, then check SOFT**). **HS 20 ⇒ constant Disadvantage**. **−3 HS per Long Rest**.

## 1) 🔢 Costs & Counters
^costs

- Track **Soul Debt (SD)** per character, starting at **1**; it **increases after each decant** per the Mode lever below.  
- **Sparkplug cost:** **Sparkplugs = SD at Start** (1 plug per point of SD).  
- Costs are **per‑character**, **never** a party pool.  
- Rare boons may **set next cost to 1** (GM’s call).


> [!mode] **Mode lever — Soul Debt (SD) accrual & cost**
> **Core (Standard):** Start **SD 1**. After each **decant** (Sparkplug or Reserve), **SD +1**. **Sparkplug cost = SD at Start**.  
> **Hardcore:** **Accrual per decant = +1 / +2 / +3** based on your **Major injuries at the moment of death** (**0–1 / 2 / 3**). *Count Majors at death; ignore injuries added at decant.* **Sparkplug cost = SD at Start**.  
> **Cost lock:** The **price locks when you press Start**; later SD changes do **not** change what you already paid.  
> **Chaos row 17 — Soul Reprieve:** **Reduce SD by 1 (min 0)**. If this hits **before Start**, it lowers that print’s price; **after Start**, it applies to **future** prints.


> [!tip] 🔌 **Sparkplugs (short)**
> Portable soul‑fuel canisters used to run instant prints, aSYNC upgrades, Hellfire boilers, and authored overclocks. **No public LEV↔PLUG market**; treat storage/sale as **story flags**.

## 2) ▶️ Starting a Print (Who/When)
^start

- 🟢 **Normal start (party):** insert plugs into bay(s), press **Start**.  
- 🚨 **TPK auto‑start:** if **all PCs are dead** and **no** plugs are inserted, 🤖 **PAVNN** auto‑starts **one Reserve print** and **locks the Train** until it completes. Then you may insert plugs (Sparkplug prints run **in parallel** per bay) **or** continue with Reserve (**sequential**).  
- 🤖 **PAVNN** = the crew’s android aide aboard the Train; fuller brief elsewhere.

## 3) ⚙️ Modes, Timings & Ops
^ops

| ⚙️ Mode | ⏱️ Per‑Body Time | 🚂 Train Ops | 🔀 Concurrency |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 🔌 **Sparkplug** | **8 h** | **Normal** (move/craft OK) | **Parallel** (per active bay) |
| 🔋 **Reserve** | **8 h + 2d4** | **Low‑Energy**: life support only; **no movement/crafting** | **Sequential** (one body at a time) |

- 🔁 **Mixing rule:** If **any** active print is **Reserve**, the Train is **Low‑Energy** **and** **sequential**. You **cannot** start Sparkplug prints until **no** Reserve print is active.  
- 💥 **Interruptions:** a major attack or **Surgefront** adds **+1d4 h** to the **current** print (**DM’s call**).  
- 🛌❌ **Printing isn’t a rest.** 🛌✅ Other PCs may rest while prints run; the printed character does **not** benefit from rests during printing.

^bays

- **Sparkplug prints** can run **in parallel** across bays.  
- **Reserve prints** are **always sequential** (**1×1**).

## 5) 🎒 Gear, Drop & Re‑equip
^gear

- On death, **all gear** drops **where you died** (or to the nearest safe, walkable spill).  
- You decant with **basic clothing only**; re‑equip from field/stores.  
- **Printing time isn’t a rest** for the printed body.

## 6) 🩸 Injuries at Decant
^injury

- **Start** at **0 Minor / 0 Major**, then apply print injuries:  
  - 🔌 **Sparkplug print:** **1d4 − 1 Minors** (**0–3**).  
  - 🔋 **Reserve print:** **+1 Major**.  
- **3 Minors = 1 Major**; **max 3 Majors** (see Injury rules).

## 7) ⚡ Hellstatic — Soul‑Shell Desync
^hs

- **Active only** while you have **3 Major injuries**; **Dormant** below 3 (**no** HS checks). HS persists as a **value**.  
- **Gain HS:** each time you **die while at 3 Majors**, **HS +3**. Track unbounded; for checks use **effective HS = min(HS, 20)**.

**Procedure (when Active):**
1) 🗣️ Declare the **specific action/test** (incl. reactions).  
2) 🎲 Roll a **Hellstatic d20**.  
3) ↔️ If the roll is **≤ effective HS**, your declared test is at **HARD Disadvantage**.  
4) ♾️ If **effective HS = 20**, you have **constant Disadvantage** (skip step 2).  
5) 🛡️ **Override semantics:** HS is a **Hard Override**: it **ignores any Soft Advantage**; **HARD vs HARD = Normal**; then resolve **SOFT**, then roll. The chosen state **persists** through rerolls of the **same test**.

+ **Decay:** At the **end of each Long Rest**, **HS −3** (min **0**), whether **Active** or **Dormant**. **Chaos row 22 (“Instant long rest”) counts for this HS decay** (it is a long rest), but it is **not Trueflow** and **does not** move **CDR** or satisfy **Trueflow‑only** recovery (e.g., injury care, Strain).

> [!optional] 🧪 **Spellcasting parity (table option)**
> If HS procs and your declared action **doesn’t use your own d20** (e.g., a spell forcing saves), choose **one**:  
> • 🎯 **Targets’ Advantage:** the **first** save caused by **your** effect this turn has **Advantage**; **or**  
> • 📉 **DC penalty:** **−2** to your Spell Save DC for that effect.

## 8) ✅ Acceptance checks
^checks

- **First death free:** At **Death# = 1**, **Sparkplug cost = 0**; at **Death# = 4**, cost = **3 plugs**.  
- **Mixing lock:** If **one** body is on **Reserve** and **two** on **Sparkplug** are queued, the Train remains **Low‑Energy** and **sequential** until **no** Reserve is active (you **cannot** start the Sparkplug jobs).  
- **TPK safety:** Party dies with **0 plugs** → **PAVNN** auto‑starts **Reserve (8 h + 2d4)**; first PC decants with **+1 Major**. After decant, insert plugs to run remaining prints **in parallel** (per bay) or continue **Reserve** (sequential; each **8 h + 2d4**, **+1 Major**).  
- **HS comparator:** With **HS 7**, a roll of **7** on HS **procs** (≤) and sets **HARD Disadvantage** on that test even if you had **Help** (Soft Advantage).  
- **HS 20 constant:** At **HS 20**, you always roll at **HARD Disadvantage** (skip HS roll).  
- **Rerolls:** If a feature lets you **reroll the d20 for the same test**, the **HARD** state from HS **persists**; then re‑apply **Offsets** (e.g., Injury dice).  
- **Printing ≠ rest:** The printed PC does **not** finish a Short/Long Rest during printing; **other** PCs may rest normally.  
- **Injury apply:** Sparkplug decant **1d4−1 Minors (0–3)**; Reserve decant **+1 Major**; **3 Minors → 1 Major**; **max 3 Majors** throttle.

## 9) 🧪 Examples
^examples

> [!example]+ 🧟 **TPK with 0 Sparkplugs**
> All PCs die with **0 plugs**. 🤖 **PAVNN** auto‑starts **Reserve** (**8 h + 2d4**): 🔒 Train locks; first PC decants with **+1 Major**. After decant, insert plugs to run remaining prints **in parallel** (per bay) or continue **Reserve** (sequential; each **8 h + 2d4**, **+1 Major**). **HS** doesn’t increase unless a PC died **while already at 3 Majors**.

> [!example]+ 🔌 **Sparkplug chain**
> A PC at **2 Majors** dies (**Death# = 4 ⇒ 3 plugs**). Sparkplug print takes **8 h** and inflicts **1d4 − 1 Minor**. If this creates **3 Majors**, **HS becomes Active**. On later deaths at **3 Majors**, **HS +3** each time.

> [!example]+ ⚡ **HS vs Help vs Reroll**
> You declare a **SYS hack** with **Help** (Soft Advantage) while **HS is Active**. HS roll **≤ HS** → **HARD Disadvantage**; **HARD** cancels **SOFT** → **Normal**. A relic then grants a **HARD Advantage**; **HARD vs HARD = Normal**; resolve **SOFT** (none left) → **Normal**. You use a feature to **reroll** the same test’s d20: the **state** (Normal) **persists**; re‑roll any **variable Offsets** (e.g., injury die).
